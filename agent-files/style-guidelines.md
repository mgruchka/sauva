Typography
Rounded sans: Quicksand, 500, 600 and 700. It is much rounder than Nunito and is free on Google Fonts. Use it for the tagline, buttons, labels, menus and short UI text.
Long text: Quicksand is light at small sizes. If you find the tables and long paragraphs tiring to read, use M PLUS Rounded 1c (400/500/700) for body text and keep Quicksand for labels and headings.
Headings and wordmark: Source Serif 4, 600–700, unchanged.
Sizes: H1 40/48 px, H2 32/40, H3 24/32, body 16/24, table or small text 14/20, caption or tagline 12 px with uppercase and +0.15em letter-spacing. For Quicksand body text, use 16 px at weight 500 for better legibility.
